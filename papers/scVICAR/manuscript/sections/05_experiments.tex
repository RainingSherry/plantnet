\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Evidence Boundary and Datasets}

The earlier NeighborMix and RG experiments on 16 datasets informed method
development and are reported only in the supplement.  They are not combined
with confirmatory inference.  The confirmatory cohort contains six datasets not
used to choose the fixed or topology-adaptive settings (Table~\ref{tab:data}).
We remove labels whose normalized value is \texttt{unknown}, \texttt{nan}, or
\texttt{unassigned}, followed by classes with fewer than ten cells.  The
unfiltered-label analysis is retained as sensitivity analysis.  The
\texttt{hrvatin\_geo} artifact is excluded because its label semantics are
invalid for formal aggregation and it is not independent of \texttt{hrvatin}.

Each canonical H5AD is immutable and identified by SHA-256.  The manifest
records source path, label field, filtering, dimensions, size, creation time,
and remote location.  Blood--Bone Marrow derives from the hematopoietic
reference map~\cite{triana2021hematopoietic}; Human Pancreas 1 is from Enge et
al.~\cite{enge2017pancreas}; PRJNA895163 is the cassava tuberous-root atlas of
Song et al.~\cite{song2022cassava}; and the Tabula Sapiens subset derives from
the multi-organ atlas~\cite{tabulasapiens2022atlas}.  Baron human/mouse pancreas
citations and processing-stage counts are detailed in
Section~\ref{sec:downstream} and the supplement.
Per-cell labels do not enter clean-view optimization,
graph construction, checkpoint selection, or run scheduling.  They are exposed
only to post-training evaluation and prespecified diagnostic interventions.

\subsection{Matched Variants and Baselines}

We compare six variants under the common backbone in Section~\ref{sec:method}:
NoMix, random mixing, scVICAR-F, topology edge-only, topology gate-only, and
full scVICAR-T.  NoMix is the matched scMAE objective; random mixing tests
whether arbitrary convex corruption explains any gain.  Component variants
separate neighbor affinity from cell-wise perturbation strength.

External references comprise PCA+KMeans, the original scMAE implementation,
scVI, scDeepCluster, scDCC, scRCL, and graph/deep-clustering methods supported by
the benchmark adapters~\cite{lopez2018scvi,tian2019scdeepcluster,tian2021scdcc,peng2024scrcl}.
Historical values are reused only when input SHA-256 and every evaluation field
match protocol v1; otherwise the baseline is rerun.  Method-family conclusions
are based on the matched variants, avoiding attribution of backbone changes to
vicinal recovery.  Every rerun baseline must emit cell identifiers that match
the frozen H5AD row-for-row before it can receive a completion marker.  The
published architectures of scDCC, scDeepCluster, and scRCL require $K$ during
clustering-oriented training; we therefore mark them explicitly as known-$K$
training baselines.  Per-cell reference labels are withheld from their gradient
updates and checkpoint selection.

\subsection{Evaluation and Frozen Hyperparameters}

Model seeds are 42, 2024, and 3407.  The six datasets, six matched variants,
and three seeds give \FormalRunCount{} primary runs.  The primary endpoint is
ARI from known-$K$ KMeans with \texttt{n\_init=20}.  NMI, assignment accuracy,
macro-F1, silhouette, runtime, and peak GPU memory are secondary.  Fixed
resolution 1.0 Leiden is reported separately; label-guided resolution sweeps
are prohibited.  Known $K$ is an evaluation assumption and is never used by the
encoder objective of the six matched scVICAR variants.  This statement does not
apply to the three external known-$K$ training baselines identified above.

The graph uses $k=5$, four sampled neighbors, $\tau=0.2$, and a maximum gate of
0.1.  All models train for 80 epochs with batch size 256, learning rate
$10^{-3}$, mask ratio 0.4, and vicinal weight 0.3 where applicable.  No
per-dataset tuning is performed.  Contrastive weight is zero, uncertainty is
disabled, and no dynamic graph, learned gate, or explicit consistency loss is
part of the primary model.

\subsection{Graph-Contamination and Estimator Tests}

On Blood--Bone Marrow, Human Pancreas 3, and PRJNA895163, we replace 0, 25, 50,
75, or 100\% of each graph row with deliberately cross-label neighbors.  Labels
are used only to construct this controlled stress intervention, never in the
clean experiment.  We compare scVICAR-F with scVICAR-T by absolute performance
and degradation from 0\% contamination.  A separate aggregation ablation
compares the implemented sampling-then-reweighting estimator in
Eq.~\eqref{eq:realized-kernel}, an unbiased uniform average of sampled
neighbors, and full-neighborhood aggregation.

For post-hoc diagnosis, we compute Spearman association and AUROC between
affinity/gate scores and same-type edge purity.  We also relate gate magnitude
to class frequency and per-class recall.  These diagnostic labels do not alter
the graph, model, or chosen checkpoint.

\subsection{Statistical Analysis}

Dataset is the independent unit.  For downstream tasks, split seeds are first
averaged within model run; model seeds are then averaged within dataset.  The
three prespecified contrasts are F--NoMix, T--NoMix, and T--F.  We report paired
dataset-level mean and median effects, win/tie/loss counts, hierarchical
bootstrap 95\% confidence intervals, paired sign-flip permutation tests, and
Holm-adjusted $p$-values.  Cells, splits, and seeds are not treated as
independent biological replicates, and no best seed is selected.

\subsection{Reproducibility and Traceability}

Every run directory is keyed by dataset, variant, seed, and a hash of the fully
resolved configuration.  It contains data and code digests, frozen parameters,
metrics, compressed float32 embeddings, clusters, topology diagnostics,
resource logs, a file-level checksum manifest, and a completion marker.  Upload
is staged in a partial remote directory, verified, and atomically promoted.
Complete hashes are skipped; no synchronization uses destructive deletion.
Every number and figure source in the manuscript resolves to one or more unique
run identifiers.
