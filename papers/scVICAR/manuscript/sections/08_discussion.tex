\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

scVICAR treats local cell structure as a corruption distribution rather than a
source of pseudo-labels.  The original anchor remains the target, and the graph
is absent at clean inference.  This design makes the local perturbation easy to
bound and gives a clear distinction between fixed vicinal regularization and
topology-adaptive risk control.

Several limitations are fundamental.  First, a PCA cosine KNN graph remains an
imperfect biological proxy.  Mutual and shared-neighbor evidence may correlate
with edge purity, but the affinity is not calibrated and may fail around
transitions, batches, or rare types.  Second, similarity and cosine distance are
algebraically redundant in the current kernel; a future implementation should
either combine their coefficient or introduce an independent distance signal.
Third, the primary sampler draws by an affinity and reweights by that affinity,
which sharpens the effective kernel.  We preserve this behavior for fidelity to
the developed model and evaluate unbiased/full-neighborhood alternatives as
ablations.

The theory describes the augmentation operator, not a universal clustering
guarantee.  Its Lipschitz conclusion depends on an unspecified encoder constant,
and the Taylor interpretation requires smoothness, small perturbations, and a
small input-direction gradient or centered perturbation; parameter stationarity
alone is insufficient.  The contamination bound becomes useful only if the
topology mechanism empirically reduces erroneous weight mass, or contracts the
overall perturbation through the gate, under the stated radius conditions.

The evaluation also has scope limits.  Known-$K$ KMeans measures embedding
quality under a supplied cluster count; fixed-resolution Leiden is included to
show a non-oracle view but does not solve resolution selection.  Six
confirmatory datasets are stronger evidence than the development table, yet
they do not cover all tissues, platforms, disease states, or batch structures.
Low-label probes are transductive and cannot be interpreted as cross-study
annotation.  Marker evaluation inherits limitations of Wilcoxon ranking and
reference-label quality.

Finally, the historical 16-dataset experiments participated in method
development.  We disclose that selection history in the supplement and keep it
separate from confirmation.  scVICAR-T is not claimed to beat scVICAR-F on
every dataset; its scientific question is whether topology adaptation provides
measurable protection when local graphs are contaminated.
