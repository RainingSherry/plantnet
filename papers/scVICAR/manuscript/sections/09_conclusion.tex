\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented scVICAR, a graph-vicinal anchor-recovery framework for masked
single-cell representation learning.  A fixed variant introduces bounded local
corruption, while a topology-adaptive variant modulates neighbor affinity and
cell-wise perturbation strength.  The unified formulation exposes convex-hull,
diffusion, and contamination properties and separates strict derivations from a
conditional local-risk interpretation.  The accompanying protocol combines
matched-backbone confirmation, controlled graph stress, and leakage-resistant
downstream evaluation.  The final conclusion about empirical benefit will
follow the complete frozen evidence: fixed recovery is judged against NoMix,
and topology adaptation is judged primarily by robustness rather than presumed
universal superiority.
